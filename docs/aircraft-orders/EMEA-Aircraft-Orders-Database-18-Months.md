## AIRCRAFT INTERIORS OPPORTUNITY ANALYSIS

### By Aircraft Type

**Single-Aisle (Narrowbody): ~1,100 aircraft**
- **Seats per aircraft:** 150-240 seats (avg ~180)
- **Total Seats:** ~198,000 seats
- **Interiors scope:**
  - Economy class seating (majority)
  - Galleys (2-4 per aircraft)
  - Lavatories (2-4 per aircraft)
  - Overhead bins
  - Mood lighting
  - IFE (varies by carrier)

**Twin-Aisle (Widebody): ~825 aircraft**
- **Seats per aircraft:** 250-400 seats (avg ~300)
- **Total Seats:** ~247,500 seats
- **Interiors scope:**
  - Multi-class seating (First, Business, Premium Economy, Economy)
  - Multiple galleys (6-12 per aircraft)
  - Multiple lavatories (8-12 per aircraft)
  - Crew rest areas
  - Premium IFE systems (typically standard)
  - Larger overhead bins
  - Advanced mood lighting

### By Carrier Type

**Legacy Carriers: ~1,033 aircraft**
- **Premium products:** Business class, First class (select carriers)
- **IFE:** Typically installed across all classes
- **Galleys:** Full-service capability
- **Seat pitch:** Higher in all classes
- **Materials:** Premium finishes
- **Customization:** High degree of brand differentiation

**Low-Cost Carriers: ~455+ aircraft**
- **Single class:** All-economy configuration
- **Seat density:** Higher (typically 180-240 seats vs 150-180)
- **IFE:** Often BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) or no IFE
- **Galleys:** Minimal, optimized for quick turnarounds
- **Lavatories:** Minimum required
- **Weight optimization:** Critical for fuel efficiency
- **Cost focus:** Value engineering essential

### Regional Insights

**Europe (excl UK): ~850+ aircraft**
- Mix of legacy and LCC (high LCC penetration)
- Stricter environmental regulations
- Focus on sustainability (materials, weight reduction)
- Short-to-medium haul focus (single-aisle dominant)
- Retrofit market significant (older fleet)

**Middle East: ~1,000+ aircraft**
- Premium carrier dominance (Emirates, Qatar, Etihad)
- High percentage of widebody (long-haul focus)
- Luxury products and differentiation
- Growing LCC segment (flynas, Air Arabia, flydubai)
- New airline launches (Riyadh Air)

**Africa: ~75+ aircraft**
- Ethiopian Airlines dominance
- Widebody focus (long-haul to Asia, Europe, Americas)
- Fleet modernization (A350 family)
- Growth potential significant

## KEY TAKEAWAYS FOR AIRCRAFT INTERIORS

### Market Size
- **1,925+ aircraft** ordered in EMEA (excl UK) in past 18 months
- **~445,500 total seats** across all aircraft
- **Delivery timeline:** 2024-2033+ (peak 2025-2029)

### Product Mix Opportunity
- **57% single-aisle:** Economy focus, high-density, weight-sensitive
- **43% twin-aisle:** Multi-class, premium products, advanced features

### Customer Segmentation
- **42% Legacy carriers:** Premium products, full IFE, customization
- **58% LCC:** Value engineering, weight optimization, standardization

### Regional Priorities
1. **Middle East (52%):** Premium widebody focus + growing LCC
2. **Europe (44%):** Balanced mix, LCC dominant in narrowbody
3. **Africa (4%):** Widebody growth, fleet modernization

### Fastest-Growing Segments
1. **Middle East LCC:** flynas (+63% capacity), Air Arabia, flydubai
2. **European LCC:** Wizz Air (434 A321neo backlog), Ryanair (300 737 MAX)
3. **Premium Long-Haul:** Qatar (184 A350), Emirates (80 widebody), Turkish (70 A350)

### Technology Trends
- **BYOD IFE:** Growing in LCC segment
- **Weight reduction:** Critical for all carriers (fuel costs)
- **Sustainability:** Recycled materials, circular economy
- **Customization:** Legacy carriers seeking differentiation
- **Quick turnaround:** LCC galley and lavatory optimization
