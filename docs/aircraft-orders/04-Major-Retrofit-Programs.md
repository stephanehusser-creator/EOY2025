## RETROFIT MARKET OPPORTUNITY

### By Region

| Region | Existing Fleet 2025 | Typical Retrofit Cycle | Annual Retrofit Market |
|--------|-------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Europe (excl UK) | 4,200 | 5-8 years | 525-840 aircraft/year |
| Middle East | 1,400 | 7-10 years | 140-200 aircraft/year |
| Africa | 650 | 8-10 years | 65-81 aircraft/year |
| **TOTAL** | **6,250** | **~7 years avg** | **730-1,121 aircraft/year** |

### By Aircraft Type

**Legacy Carriers - Typical Retrofit Scope:**
- Business class: Full replacement every 7-10 years
- Premium economy: Refresh every 5-8 years
- Economy: Refresh every 8-10 years
- Galleys/Lavatories: Refresh every 10-15 years
- IFE: Technology upgrade every 5-7 years

**LCC - Typical Retrofit Scope:**
- Economy seats: Replacement every 10-12 years
- Minimal galley/lavatory refresh
- No IFE refresh (BYOD model)

- Legacy carriers: More frequent and comprehensive retrofits
