**EMEA Total Fleet 2025-2035:**

**Key Insight:** LCC growing 14% faster than legacy carriers, gaining market share from 39% to 40%

## Strategic Insights for Interiors Suppliers

### Target Customer Priorities

**Tier 1 - High Value:**
- Emirates, Qatar Airways, Etihad (premium widebody)
- Turkish Airlines (large volume, both types)
- Lufthansa Group (legacy quality, volume)

**Tier 2 - High Volume:**
- Ryanair, Wizz Air (LCC narrowbody, standardized)
- flynas (fastest-growing LCC in ME)
- Air France-KLM (legacy quality)

**Tier 3 - Growth Opportunity:**
- Ethiopian Airlines (Africa growth leader)
- EgyptAir (regional growth)
- Saudia, Riyadh Air (Saudi Vision 2030)

### Product Strategy

**For Legacy Carriers:**
✅ Premium multi-class products
✅ Advanced IFE systems
✅ Customization and differentiation
✅ Retrofit and upgrade services

**For LCC:**
✅ Lightweight economy seats
✅ High-density configurations
✅ Cost-competitive standardized products
✅ BYOD or no-IFE solutions
✅ Quick delivery and installation

**For All:**
✅ Sustainability credentials
✅ Weight reduction technologies
✅ Regulatory compliance (EASA, etc.)
✅ Aftermarket support

### Regional Strategies

**Europe (excl UK):**
- Focus: Mix of premium and LCC
- Compliance: EASA, sustainability mandates
- Volume: 1,800-2,000 deliveries
- Key: Balance premium differentiation with LCC efficiency

**Middle East:**
- Focus: Premium widebody + growing LCC narrowbody
- Differentiation: Luxury and innovation
- Volume: 2,200-2,500 deliveries
- Key: Premium product leadership, capture new airline launches

**Africa:**
- Focus: Widebody for long-haul
- Position: Early engagement for growth
- Volume: 450-500 deliveries
- Key: Partnership with Ethiopian, EgyptAir
