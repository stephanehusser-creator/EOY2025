## Key Takeaways

After two fantastic years of strong performance, Emirates orders were significantly reduced in 2024. This reduction is primarily due to:

- **Fleet Retrofit Completion**: Most aircraft equipped with our products have already been retrofitted to new condition during the previous two exceptional years
- **Maintenance Cycle Timeline**: Major maintenance orders are not expected to resume until approximately 3 years after the retrofit completion, as the newly installed equipment requires minimal servicing during this initial period

This cyclical pattern is a natural consequence of the successful large-scale retrofit program and should be factored into future planning and forecasting.

### Strategic Account Priorities

#### Program Overview – Block-by-Block Commentary

**1. Major Legacy Carrier – Lufthansa Group**

We continue to work closely with Lufthansa Group and their design partners. We are already selected on the 787 and A350 programs, with the integration work done through Stelia and Collins, so these programs are essentially secured.

**2. Turkish Airlines**

Turkish is primarily managed through TCI, which is a challenging partner to work with. We have been collaborating with them on the A3 business class seat, and they are now inquiring about ultra-leather for economy class seat covers. This is still exploratory.

**3. Air France**

We are well positioned with Air France. Our current work focuses on improving treatments to enhance comfort, appearance, and perceived quality. Our position is stable and positive.

**4. SAS**

SAS focuses mostly on single-aisle aircraft, meaning no real opportunities for vertical surfaces and therefore limited relevance for our vertical product lines.

**5. TAP Air Portugal**

Similar to SAS, TAP operates mostly single-aisle programs, so again no vertical surface business at this stage.

**6. Iberia**

Iberia is in the middle of an ongoing program cycle. Because everything is locked in, there is no entry point for us at the moment.

**7. ITA Airways**

ITA is considered lost as a program for us. No further opportunities expected short-term.

**8. Croatia Airlines**

Croatia is also focused on single-aisle, thus no vertical product opportunity.

**9. Icelandair**

We removed Icelandair from the priority list. As a low-cost carrier, they do not present a meaningful opportunity for our product range.

**10. Emirates**

Emirates is expected to launch a new interior definition, but nothing has been finalized yet. We are monitoring the situation and preparing positioning accordingly.

**11. Qatar Airways**

This is one of the key strategic targets. We are working on the business class single line, and our aim is to secure a stronger position on the A350 program, where we currently are present only with Ultrasuede. We expect a larger opportunity once the A350 interior definition evolves.

**12. Etihad Airways**

Etihad has been a very positive development. We currently hold most of the major surfaces, including the larger surface areas. They initially selected Nomex, but then switched and requested Suede instead between September and October. We need to clarify this shift during the next visit, but overall it is an excellent position for us.

**13. Saudia**

For the 787, we are well positioned, with some Ultra Leather already in use. The next objective is to secure the PANS (panels / vertical surfaces).

**14. flynas**

flynas represents a significant potential opportunity next year. They currently use Peron, but based on our interactions, there may be a real opening for us to break into their seat cover program. This could become one of the largest seat-cover opportunities for us.

**15. Ethiopian Airlines**

Ethiopian would be an extremely strategic addition to the portfolio. We are still seeking an entry point, but securing Ethiopian in the coming months or years would be a major milestone.

### Major Retrofit Programs

**1. Emirates ⭐ (Largest Retrofit Program)**

- **Investment:** $5 billion USD
- **Aircraft:** Boeing 777 fleet + A380 fleet (~260 passenger aircraft)
- **Timeline:** 2024–2030+
- **Scope:**
  - Premium cabin upgrades
  - Economy refresh
  - IFE modernization
  - Galley/lavatory updates

**Key Highlights:**
- The 777 fleet retrofit is nearing completion
- The A380 upgrade is the major upcoming program
- Original A380 business class is heavy on paint/laminate, less ergonomic and less premium than current Boeing cabins
- The retrofit will bring the A380 in line with Boeing fleet standards
- Expect reduced first-class seats but more opportunities for our premium materials
- New business class will be lighter, more ergonomic, and visually upscale

**Status:** Ongoing

**2. Qatar Airways**

- **Aircraft:** A350 fleet (21 in-service, 184 on order)
- **Scope:** Cabin refresh and retrofit for older aircraft

**Key Highlights:**
- Aligning retrofit with LineFit products where possible
- Opportunity to extend our presence across both new and existing fleet

**Status:** Ongoing
