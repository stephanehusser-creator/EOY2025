## Key Takeaways

After two fantastic years of strong performance, Emirates orders were significantly reduced in 2024. This reduction is primarily due to:

- **Fleet Retrofit Completion**: Most aircraft equipped with our products have already been retrofitted to new condition during the previous two exceptional years
- **Maintenance Cycle Timeline**: Major maintenance orders are not expected to resume until approximately 3 years after the retrofit completion, as the newly installed equipment requires minimal servicing during this initial period

This cyclical pattern is a natural consequence of the successful large-scale retrofit program and should be factored into future planning and forecasting.
