### Competitive Intelligence Insights

#### Missed Opportunities Analysis

**SAS (Scandinavian Airlines)**
- Lost primarily to laminate suppliers
- Key competitive disadvantage: Laminates offer lower minimum order quantities (MOQs) better suited for small batch production
- Lesson: Need to evaluate flexible production capabilities for smaller batch orders to compete in this segment

**TAP Air Portugal**
- Lost at the design level to a local Portuguese designer
- Customer preference for local design partnerships
- Assessment: Outcome would likely have been similar regardless of our approach
- Lesson: Early engagement in design phase critical; local partnerships may be necessary in certain markets

**Ethiopian Airlines**
- Extremely challenging market to penetrate
- Very limited opportunities for direct engagement and meetings
- Access barriers significantly restrict business development efforts
- Lesson: Requires sustained long-term relationship building strategy; alternative engagement channels needed

**EgyptAir**
- Identified as a genuine missed opportunity
- Represents the primary case where different approach could have yielded results
- Lesson: Requires detailed analysis of what went wrong and actionable improvements for similar future opportunities
