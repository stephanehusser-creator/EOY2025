## Performance Analysis

### Major Wins

#### Vertical Surfaces
- **Complete Success**: All vertical surface opportunities secured except KLM (still in process)
- Strong market position maintained in this segment
- Consistent delivery and quality standards driving customer confidence

#### Unexpected Wins
- **FlyDubai Armrest & Headrest**: Surprise win demonstrating the power of design partnerships
  - Customer initially sought confirmation on seat cover performance
  - JPA design partnership successfully influenced expanded Ultraleather application
  - Highlights importance of design house relationships and product performance validation

### Lost Opportunities and Competitive Losses

#### Seat Cover Losses
- **Lufthansa Seat Covers**: Only loss in seat cover category
  - **Primary Factor**: Sustainability requirements
  - **Strategic Implication**: Need to strengthen environmental credentials and certifications
  - **Market Signal**: Growing importance of sustainability in material selection decisions

### Pipeline Opportunities

#### Active Opportunities
- **KLM**: Vertical surfaces opportunity still in negotiation
- **Fly NAs**: Potential new seat cover opportunity
- **Turkish Airlines**: New seat cover opportunity identified

### Notes on Seat Cover Market Dynamics

#### Key Market Trends
- Seat covers remain highly competitive with sustainability as critical decision factor
- Performance validation essential for customer confidence (demonstrated by FlyDubai case)
- Design partnerships (like JPA) can significantly influence product application expansion

#### Strategic Considerations
1. **Sustainability Imperative**: Lufthansa loss underscores need for enhanced environmental credentials
2. **Performance Proof Points**: Customer confidence depends on demonstrable product performance
3. **Design Partner Leverage**: JPA collaboration shows value of design house relationships
4. **New Market Development**: Fly NAs and Turkish Airlines represent growth potential in seat cover segment

#### Action Items
- [ ] Enhance sustainability documentation and certifications for seat cover products
- [ ] Develop case studies from successful seat cover applications
- [ ] Strengthen relationships with design houses like JPA Design
- [ ] Follow up aggressively on KLM vertical surface opportunity
