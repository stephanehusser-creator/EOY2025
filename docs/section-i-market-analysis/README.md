## Key Metrics Summary

### EMEA Market Overview
- **Infrastructure Market:** $1.03B (2025), CAGR 4.42% (2025-2033)
- **MEA Aviation Market:** $34.43B (2025)
- **Middle East Market:** $28.38B (2025) → $35.19B (2030), CAGR 4.40%
- **Regional Position:** 9% of global pre-owned aircraft transactions

### European Performance (2024)
- **Total Passengers:** 2.5B+ (+7.3% YoY, +1.8% vs 2019)
- **International Traffic:** +8.8% vs 2023
- **Domestic Traffic:** +2.5% vs 2023, -6.3% vs 2019
- **Recovery Status:** 47% of airports below pre-pandemic levels

### Middle East & Africa (2024)
- **Middle East Growth:** +5% since 2019 (2nd fastest globally)
- **RPK Growth:** +33.3% YoY
- **Cargo Growth:** +19.9% YoY
- **Africa Capacity:** 24.2M seats (+8.6% YoY)

### Segment Breakdown
- **Commercial Aviation:** 72% of EMEA market
- **Military Aviation:** Qatar leading with 24% market share
- **LCC Penetration:** 29% of Middle East capacity
- **Aviation Fuels:** $12.98B → $26.12B (2024-2030), CAGR 12.19%
