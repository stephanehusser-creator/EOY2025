## 7. SUMMARY: 2026 MARKET OUTLOOK

### Overall Assessment: **POSITIVE GROWTH** 🟢

**Key Highlights:**
- **225 aircraft net fleet growth** in 2026 (+3.6%)
- **473 new aircraft deliveries** expected
- **Middle East leading** at +7.1% annual growth (2.5x global average)
- **105M additional passengers** across EMEA (+5.5%)
- **Strong orderbook:** 1,925+ aircraft to be delivered through 2035

**Regional Momentum:**
- 🟢 **Middle East:** Exceptional growth (+7.1% fleet, +8.6% passengers)
- 🟡 **Europe:** Steady recovery (+2.4% fleet, +3.9% passengers)
- 🟢 **Africa:** Moderate growth (+3.8% fleet, +6.3% passengers)

**Carrier Type Trends:**
- **LCC growing faster** than legacy (+4.3% vs +3.2%)
- **LCC market share stable** at ~39% of EMEA fleet
- **Twin-aisle deliveries strong** in Middle East (hub strategies)
