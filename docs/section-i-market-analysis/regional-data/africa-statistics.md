## EAST AFRICA

### Overview
- **Fleet 2024:** 220 aircraft
- **Growth 2019-2024:** +15.4% (fastest in Africa)
- **Leader:** Ethiopian Airlines (Pan-African strategy)
- **Characteristics:** Hub-based connectivity

### Ethiopia

**Ethiopian Airlines:**
- **Fleet 2024:** ~150 aircraft
- **Orders:** 58 total orderbook (14 A350: 11x -900, 3x -1000)
- **Milestone:** First A350-1000 delivered Nov 2024 (Africa's first)
- **Vision 2035:** Double fleet to 270 aircraft
- **Strategy:** Pan-African network, hub development
- **Network:** 130+ destinations (extensive African coverage)

**Addis Ababa Hub:**
- **Status:** Bole International Airport
- **Passengers:** 17M+ (2024)
- **Role:** Primary Pan-African connecting point
- **Development:** Expansion ongoing

### Kenya

**Kenya Airways:**
- **Fleet:** ~35 aircraft
- **Status:** Fleet renewal, network rebuild
- **Hub:** Nairobi (Jomo Kenyatta International)
- **Focus:** East Africa regional hub, safari tourism

**Other:**
- Multiple smaller regional carriers
- Tourism-driven demand (safari destinations)
- Infrastructure development ongoing

### Tanzania, Uganda, Rwanda
- **Fleet:** Combined ~35 aircraft
- **Status:** Growing tourism sectors
- **Infrastructure:** Airport improvements
- **Opportunity:** Emerging markets

## SINGLE AFRICAN AIR TRANSPORT MARKET (SAATM)

### Overview
- **Launch:** 2018
- **Signatories:** 35+ African countries
- **Goal:** Open skies across Africa, liberalized air transport

### Status (2024)
- **Implementation:** Slow progress
- **Challenges:**
  - Regulatory fragmentation
  - Protectionism in some markets
  - Limited infrastructure
  - Bilateral agreements resistance

### Potential Impact
- **If fully implemented:**
  - 155,000+ new jobs
  - $1.3B+ in annual GDP impact
  - Increased connectivity
  - Lower fares
  - LCC expansion opportunity

**Current:** Limited impact due to slow implementation
