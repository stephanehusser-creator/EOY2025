## MAJOR AIRLINES

### Legacy Carriers

**Lufthansa Group (Germany):**
- Fleet 2024: ~756 aircraft (Group: Lufthansa, Swiss, Austrian, Brussels, Eurowings)
- Orders: 107 (40 737-8, 40 A220-300, 7 787-9, 15 A350-1000, 5 A350-900)
- Status: Allegris retrofit program ongoing

**Air France-KLM (France):**
- Fleet 2024: ~553 aircraft (Air France, KLM, Transavia)
- Orders: 150+ (100 A320neo family, 50 A350 family)
- Status: 777/A350 cabin refresh ongoing

**Turkish Airlines (Turkey):**
- Fleet 2024: ~418 aircraft
- Orders: 220 (150 A321neo, 70 A350)
- Status: Fastest growing legacy carrier in Europe (+23% 2019-2024)

**SAS (Scandinavia):**
- Fleet 2024: ~130 aircraft
- Orders: 53 (45 E195-E2, 6 A320neo, 2 A350-900)
- Status: Fleet transformation (all Boeing 737s retired Nov 2023)

**Iberia (Spain):**
- Fleet 2024: ~135 aircraft
- Orders: Ongoing A350-900 deliveries, first A321XLR operator (Oct 2024)
- Status: Modernization focus

**TAP Air Portugal:**
- Fleet 2024: ~100 aircraft
- Orders: 26 (24 A320neo family, 2 A330-900)
- Status: Fleet renewal

**ITA Airways (Italy):**
- Fleet 2024: ~100 aircraft
- Orders: 5 A330neo delivered 2024
- Status: Successor to Alitalia, rebuilding

### Low-Cost Carriers

**Ryanair (Ireland):**
- Fleet 2024: ~570 aircraft (737-800, 737 MAX)
- Orders: 150 firm (737 MAX-10, delivery 2027-2033) + 150 options
- Status: Europe's largest LCC, high-density configs (197+ seats)

**Wizz Air (Hungary):**
- Fleet 2024: ~168 aircraft

- Status: Rapid expansion, ultra-high-density (230+ seats)

**Eurowings (Germany - Lufthansa Group):**
- Fleet 2024: ~95 aircraft (A320 family)
- Status: Included in Lufthansa Group fleet

**Transavia (France/Netherlands - Air France-KLM):**
- Fleet 2024: ~80 aircraft (A320neo, 737-800)
- Status: Included in AF-KLM fleet

**Vueling (Spain - IAG):**
- Fleet 2024: ~125 aircraft (A320 family)
- Status: Spain's leading LCC

## KEY TRENDS

### Strengths
✅ Recovery to pre-pandemic levels complete
✅ Large, mature market with stable demand
✅ Strong LCC penetration and growth
✅ Infrastructure investments ongoing
✅ Tourism driving growth (Spain, Greece, Italy, Turkey)

### Challenges
⚠️ Sustainability regulations adding costs
⚠️ Workforce shortages constraining growth
⚠️ Economic uncertainty in some markets
⚠️ Airport capacity constraints (slots)
⚠️ Higher operating costs (labor, fuel, compliance)

### Opportunities
✅ Retrofit market (€345-748M annually)
✅ Sustainability solutions (weight reduction, SAF support)
✅ Fleet modernization (replacements + growth)
✅ Digital transformation (connectivity, IFE)
✅ Infrastructure projects (airports, terminals)
