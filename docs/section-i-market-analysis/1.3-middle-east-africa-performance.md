## MIDDLE EAST - COUNTRY ANALYSIS

### United Arab Emirates

**Market Position:** Regional aviation leader, global hub status

**Performance Metrics:**
- **Fleet Size 2024:** 550 aircraft (+10% vs 2019)
- **Major Carriers:** Emirates (260 aircraft), Etihad Airways, flydubai
- **Hub Airports:** Dubai International (DXB), Abu Dhabi (AUH)

**Recent Orders (18 months):**
- **Emirates:** 80 aircraft total
  - 50x Airbus A350 (twin-aisle)
  - 30x Boeing 787-9 (twin-aisle)
- **Etihad Airways:** 112 aircraft
  - 7x A320neo/A321neo (single-aisle)
  - 3x Boeing 787-9 (twin-aisle)
  - ~100 outstanding orders

**Major Programs:**
- **Emirates $5B Retrofit:** Comprehensive interior upgrade for 777 and A380 fleets (2024-2030+)
- **Dubai Airport Expansion:** DXB capacity expansion, Al Maktoum development
- **Abu Dhabi Hub Growth:** Terminal expansion, network development

**Growth Outlook 2025-2035:**
- Fleet: 550 → 750+ aircraft (+36%)
- Focus on premium long-haul services
- Hub competition with Qatar and Saudi Arabia

**Market Position:** Premium global hub, rapid growth

**Performance Metrics:**
- **Fleet Size 2024:** 250 aircraft (+8.7% vs 2019)
- **Major Carrier:** Qatar Airways (leading legacy carrier)
- **Hub Airport:** Hamad International Airport (DOH)

  - Deliveries through 2029+

**Key Strengths:**
- Premium service focus (Qsuite business class)
- Strong global network connectivity
- Modern, efficient fleet
- World Cup infrastructure legacy

**Growth Outlook 2025-2035:**
- Fleet: 250 → 350+ aircraft (+40%)
- Continued A350 deliveries dominating growth
- Focus on premium long-haul expansion

### Saudi Arabia

**Market Position:** Fastest growing market, Vision 2030 transformation

**Performance Metrics:**
- **Fleet Size 2024:** 300 aircraft (+25% vs 2019) - **Highest growth in Middle East**
- **Major Carriers:** Saudia (legacy), flynas (LCC), new Riyadh Air (launching)
- **Hub Development:** Riyadh (King Salman Airport), Jeddah expansion, Red Sea destinations

**Recent Orders (18 months):**
- **Saudia:** 39 aircraft
  - 39x Boeing 787 (twin-aisle)
  - Delivery: 2023-2029
- **flynas:** 190 aircraft - **Largest LCC order in Middle East**
  - 30x A320 (June 2023)
  - 160x Airbus single-aisle (July 2024)
  - Delivery: 2024-2029

**Major Infrastructure Projects:**
- **King Salman International Airport (Riyadh):** $31B, 120M passenger capacity by 2030
- **Red Sea Project:** 8 boutique destinations, dedicated airport
- **Jeddah Airport Expansion:** Terminal 1 redevelopment
- **NEOM:** Future aviation hub development

**Vision 2030 Impact:**
- Target: 330M passengers by 2030 (from 100M in 2019)
- Tourism sector expansion (100M tourists target)
- New airline launches (Riyadh Air with 72 aircraft on order)
- Extensive network expansion (domestic and international)

**Growth Outlook 2025-2035:**
- Fleet: 300 → 550+ aircraft (+83%) - **Highest growth in EMEA**
- Balanced legacy and LCC growth
- Infrastructure-led expansion
