## 16. COMBINED OUTLOOK SUMMARY

### Middle East 2026 Forecast

**Fleet Growth:** +100 aircraft (+7.1%) - **Fastest in EMEA**
- **Deliveries:** 240 aircraft (**highest delivery rate per capita globally**)
- **Twin-aisle focus:** 77% of deliveries (hub strategies)
- **Government backing:** Vision 2030, infrastructure mega-projects
- **Sentiment:** 🟢 **Very Positive** - Exceptional growth certainty

### Africa 2026 Forecast

**Fleet Growth:** +25 aircraft (+3.8%)
- **Deliveries:** 48 aircraft (Ethiopian leading with 20-22)
- **Twin-aisle focus:** 69% of deliveries (long-haul connectivity)
- **Long-term potential:** Ethiopian Vision 2035, infrastructure development
- **Sentiment:** 🟡 **Cautiously Positive** - Constrained by infrastructure, high long-term potential
