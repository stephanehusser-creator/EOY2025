| Factor | Impact | Evidence |

### Growth Constraints (-)

| Factor | Impact | Mitigation |
|--------|--------|------------|
| **Sustainability Regulations** | High | ReFuelEU, EU ETS adding costs; driving efficiency |
| **Workforce Shortages** | High | Pilots -18K, engineers -36K; constraining growth |
| **Airport Slot Constraints** | Medium | Capacity limits at major hubs |
| **Economic Uncertainty** | Medium | Inflation, energy costs impacting demand |

**Data Sources:** Industry workforce reports, regulatory impact assessments
