# 1.2 European Market Performance (2024)

**Section:** I - Global Status of Aviation in EMEA Zone
**Last Updated:** November 4, 2025

---

## Executive Summary

European aviation has definitively exceeded pre-pandemic levels in 2024, with **2.5 billion+ passengers** representing **+7.3% growth YoY** and **+1.8% above 2019 levels**. However, recovery remains uneven, with 47% of airports still below pre-pandemic performance and significant variance between countries and airport sizes.

---

## Overall Performance Metrics

### Passenger Traffic Recovery

**2024 Annual Performance**
- **Total Passengers:** 2.5+ billion
- **Year-over-Year Growth:** +7.3% vs 2023
- **vs Pre-Pandemic:** +1.8% vs 2019
- **Milestone:** First year to exceed 2019 total annual passengers

**Traffic Composition**
| Traffic Type | 2024 Growth (YoY) | vs 2019 |
|--------------|-------------------|---------|
| International | +8.8% | Above 2019 |
| Domestic | +2.5% | -6.3% |
| **Total** | **+7.3%** | **+1.8%** |

**Key Insight:** International traffic driving recovery, while domestic travel lags significantly behind pre-pandemic levels.

## Recovery Challenges

### Airports Still Below Pre-Pandemic

**Overall Statistics**
- **47% of European airports** remain below 2019 levels
- **Recovery Disparity:** Significant gap between large hubs and smaller airports

### Small Airport Crisis

**Airports <1 Million Passengers**
- **2024 Growth Rate:** +5% (slowest among all segments)
- **vs 2019:** Staggering **-34.5%** below pre-pandemic
- **Challenges:**
  - Route network reduction
  - Airline consolidation
  - Economic viability questions
  - Limited recovery investment

**Implications:**
- Regional connectivity gaps
- Economic impact on smaller communities
- Potential permanent capacity loss
- Need for targeted support programs

## 2025 Outlook for Europe

### Growth Forecast

**Passenger Traffic Projection**
- **2025 Growth:** +4% forecast
- **Trajectory:** Post-pandemic rebound slowing to sustainable growth
- **Expected:** Revenue and passenger numbers to surpass pre-pandemic levels

**Key Factors:**
- Economic conditions in major source markets
- Business travel recovery pace
- Tourism demand sustainability
- Airline capacity deployment
- Fuel cost impacts

### Moderating Growth Drivers

**Why Growth Slowing from +7.3% to +4%:**
1. **Base Effect:** 2024 benefited from low 2023 comparison
2. **Market Saturation:** Major hubs approaching capacity
3. **Economic Uncertainty:** Inflation, interest rates impacting discretionary travel
4. **Environmental Pressures:** Sustainability concerns, flight shame
5. **Competition:** High-speed rail expanding in Europe

## Technology and Digital Transformation

### Biometric Deployment

**Europe-Wide Initiative**
- **Target:** 75% of European airports installing biometric self-boarding gates by 2025
- **Current Status:** Active deployment phase across major hubs

**Success Case: London Heathrow**

## Sustainability Challenges

### ReFuelEU Aviation Regulation

**Requirements:**
- **2025:** 2% minimum SAF blend
- **2050:** 70% SAF blend
- **Current Production:** Only 0.53% of required fuel (2024)
- **Cost Challenge:** SAF 3-10x more expensive than conventional fuel

**Impact on European Airlines:**
- Immediate compliance costs starting 2025
- Need for SAF supply agreements
- Competitive disadvantage vs non-EU airlines on international routes

### EU Emissions Trading System (ETS)

**Phase-Out Schedule:**
- Free emissions allowances ending by 2026
- Carbon cost now **#3 largest expense** for major European airlines (after fuel, labor)

**Financial Impact:**
- Significant new cost category
- Requires carbon management strategy
- Influences route profitability calculations

### Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive

- **New EU Framework:** Standardized sustainability reporting
- **Compliance Requirements:** Additional administrative burden
- **Cost Impact:** Implementation and ongoing compliance costs

## Key Takeaways

**🎯 Recovery Complete:** Europe exceeded pre-pandemic levels (+1.8%) in 2024 with 2.5B+ passengers

**📊 Growth Moderating:** From +7.3% (2024) to expected +4% (2025) as recovery normalizes

**🌍 Uneven Performance:** Turkey/Italy/Spain thriving (+13% to +23%), Germany/France lagging (-16% to -3%)

**✈️ Hub Dominance:** London Heathrow (83.9M), Istanbul (80.1M) leading, small airports struggling (-34.5%)

**💡 Technology Leading:** Biometrics (75% deployment target), AI operations (Munich 35% efficiency gain)

**⚠️ Challenges Persist:** 47% airports below 2019, small airports crisis, sustainability costs, workforce gap

**📈 2025 Outlook:** +4% growth forecast, sustainable trajectory, revenue exceeding pre-pandemic
